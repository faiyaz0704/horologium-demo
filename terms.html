Section 03

Pricing & Payment

All prices displayed on our website are listed in Bangladeshi Taka (৳) and include applicable taxes. Prices are valid at the time of order placement and are subject to change without prior notice. A price change will not affect orders that have already been confirmed.

Accepted Payment Methods

We currently offer the following payment options:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) — pay in cash at the time of delivery. Available for orders within Bangladesh only. A verification call may be placed before dispatch for orders exceeding ৳50,000.
  • bKash — send payment to our designated bKash merchant number. Order confirmation will be sent once payment is verified, typically within one business hour.
  • Nagad — transfer to our Nagad merchant account. The same verification timeline applies as bKash.
  • Bank Transfer — direct transfer to our designated bank account. Please include your order number as the payment reference. Orders will be processed after the transfer clears, which may take one to two business days.

All prepaid orders are processed only after payment confirmation. We do not store payment credentials. For bKash and Nagad transactions, please retain your transaction ID as proof of payment.

Section 04

Shipping & Delivery

We ship all orders through our logistics partner, Steadfast Courier, ensuring reliable handling and real-time tracking for every package.

Delivery Timelines

  • Inside Dhaka — orders are typically delivered within 1–2 business days from dispatch.
  • Outside Dhaka — delivery takes approximately 3–5 business days depending on the destination district.

Business days are Saturday through Thursday. Orders placed after 3:00 PM or on Fridays will be processed on the next business day.

Shipping Fees

  • Inside Dhaka — complimentary shipping on all orders.
  • Outside Dhaka — a flat shipping fee of ৳120 applies. For orders above ৳30,000, shipping is complimentary.

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a confirmation email or SMS containing your Steadfast tracking number. You can monitor your delivery status in real time through the Track My Order page on our website.

Delivery Conditions

All shipments are insured during transit. Packages are sealed with tamper-evident packaging. Upon receiving your order, we ask that you inspect the outer packaging before accepting delivery. If the parcel shows visible signs of damage, you may refuse delivery. Please contact us immediately so we can arrange a replacement dispatch.

Section 05

Returns & Refunds

We want you to be fully satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason your timepiece does not meet your expectations, we offer a structured return process within a defined inspection window.

Inspection Window

You have 3 calendar days from the date of delivery to inspect your timepiece and initiate a return. The inspection window begins on the day you receive the product, as confirmed by Steadfast delivery records.

Conditions for Return

To be eligible for a return, the following conditions must be met:

  • The watch must be unworn and in its original condition, with no scratches, marks, or signs of use.
  • All original packaging, tags, warranty cards, and accessories must be included and undamaged.
  • The protective films, if present at the time of delivery, must remain intact.
  • A valid order number and proof of purchase must be provided.

Items that have been worn, resized, engraved, or damaged after delivery are not eligible for return. Returns initiated beyond the 3-day window will not be accepted.

Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund within 2 business days. Approved refunds will be processed using the original payment method:

  • bKash / Nagad — refund issued within 3 business days.
  • Bank Transfer — refund issued within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.
  • COD orders — refund transferred to your bKash, Nagad, or bank account of choice within 5 business days.

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the buyer, unless the return is due to a defect or an error on our part, in which case we will arrange and cover the return pickup.

Section 06

Warranty Policy

Every timepiece purchased from Horologium comes with a 2-year manufacturer warranty from the date of original purchase.

What Is Covered

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. This includes:

  • Movement malfunctions not caused by external damage or mishandling.
  • Crown, pusher, and winding mechanism defects.
  • Manufacturing defects in the dial, hands, or case construction.
  • Water resistance failure within the manufacturer's stated rating, provided the case back and crown were properly secured.

What Is Not Covered

The following are excluded from warranty coverage:

  • Normal wear and tear, including scratches on the crystal, case, or bracelet.
  • Damage resulting from impacts, drops, or exposure to extreme conditions outside the watch's stated tolerances.
  • Battery replacement for quartz movements.
  • Damage caused by unauthorised repairs, modifications, or servicing by third parties.
  • Straps, buckles, and other accessories unless specifically covered by the manufacturer.

What Voids the Warranty

The warranty is voided if the watch has been opened, serviced, or modified by anyone other than an authorised service centre. Removal or alteration of the serial number or case back markings will also void the warranty. To make a warranty claim, please contact our support team with your order number and a description of the issue. We will guide you through the process.
