The Royal Oak is a testament to the belief that a sports watch can be both refined and unapologetically bold. Introduced in 1972 by Gerald Genta, the octagonal bezel with exposed hex screws became an instant icon — a geometric statement that redefined what a luxury timepiece could be.

This particular reference pairs a striking emerald-green "Grande Tapisserie" dial with an 18-karat yellow gold case and integrated bracelet. The tapisserie pattern — a repeating grid of small, hand-finished squares — catches light at different angles, giving the dial extraordinary depth and texture.

Movement

Powered by the in-house Calibre 4302, a self-winding movement with 70 hours of power reserve. Visible through the sapphire case back, the movement features a 22K gold rotor with hand-applied decorations including perlage, Geneva stripes, and bevelled edges.

What's Included

Complete set: inner and outer box, certificate of authenticity, instruction manual, warranty card (2 years), polishing cloth, and size adjustment links.
