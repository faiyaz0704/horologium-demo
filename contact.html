Common Questions

Orders within Dhaka are typically delivered within 1–2 business days. For deliveries outside Dhaka, please allow 3–5 business days. All shipments are fully insured and include real-time tracking via Steadfast Courier.

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery, provided the timepiece is in its original condition with all packaging and documentation intact. Exchanges can be arranged for a different model of equal or greater value. Contact us to initiate the process.

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) across Bangladesh, bKash, Nagad, and direct bank transfer. Payment details are confirmed at checkout and in your order confirmation email.

Our warranty covers manufacturing defects in the movement, case, and dial for 24 months from the date of purchase. It does not cover damage from misuse, water exposure beyond the rated depth, or normal wear. Warranty service is handled locally in Dhaka.
